Quality at every stage of manufacturing.

At PAP Weld Core Automation, quality is built into every stage — from design validation and incoming material inspection to in-process checks and final testing. Our Chennai facility follows structured procedures to ensure every welding automation system and CNC machine meets performance and safety requirements.

Structured Processes

Documented quality checks at each production stage.

Continuous Improvement

Feedback from commissioning and service feeds back into design.

Our 4-Step Quality Process

A systematic approach to ensure reliability and performance of every machine.

1. Design Validation

Every system is engineered with validated designs, component selection and simulation before production.

2. Incoming Material Inspection

Raw materials and bought-out components are checked against specifications before use.

3. In-Process Checks

Fabrication, assembly and wiring are monitored at each stage with documented checkpoints.

4. Final Testing & Commissioning

Every machine is run-tested, calibrated and commissioned at the customer site.

Testing Facilities

Our facility is equipped to test welding, motion and control systems before dispatch.

Welding Trials

MIG/MAG, SAW, TIG and robotic welding trials on sample components.

Robot Calibration

6-axis robot calibration, repeatability and path accuracy verification.

CNC Accuracy Checks

Laser calibration and dimensional verification for CNC cutting and drilling machines.

Load & Safety Testing

Functional load tests, safety interlocks and PLC logic validation.
