Since 2025 PAP Weld Core Automation — Precision Welding Automation & CNC Systems.

PAP Weld Core Automation Private Limited is a Chennai-based manufacturer of welding automation equipment, robotic welding systems and CNC machines. Incorporated in May 2025, we design, build and commission complete welding automation lines for H-beam, PEB and heavy structural fabrication.

Chennai Based Manufacturer

Our factory in Vellanur, Chennai produces welding automation and CNC machinery.

In-House Engineering

From concept to commissioning, our team handles design, manufacturing and support.

Our Technology Partners

We integrate world-class robotics, welding and CNC controllers from leading global brands.

In-House Design & Manufacturing

All systems are designed and built at our Chennai facility for complete quality control.

Custom Automation Solutions

Tailored welding automation for H-beam, PEB, heavy fabrication, automotive and more.

After-Sales Support

Dedicated support across India for installation, training, spares and maintenance.

On-Time Delivery

Project-managed execution with clear timelines and rigorous quality checks.
