Welding automation for heavy fabrication industries.

PAP Weld Core Automation designs and builds welding automation and CNC systems for fabricators who work with steel structures, beams, girders, tanks, chassis and precision plate components. Our machines are used across PEB, structural steel, heavy fabrication, infrastructure, automotive and defence manufacturing.

H-Beam & PEB Fabrication

PTW, tack welding, assembly and beam welding lines.

Robotic Welding Cells

Yaskawa, ABB and Fanuc based robotic welding systems.

Beam welding lines, PTW pull-through welding, tack welding and H-beam assembly for pre-engineered building fabrication.
