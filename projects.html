How We Deliver a Project

A structured project flow from requirement study to commissioning and after-sales support.

1. Requirement Study

We review beam sizes, plate thickness, part geometry and production volume.

2. System Design

Layout, process selection and machine configuration finalised with the customer.

3. Build & Test

Fabrication, assembly and wiring at our Chennai facility with functional run-testing.

4. Commissioning

On-site installation, calibration, operator training and after-sales support.
